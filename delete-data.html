DELETE YOUR ONE KEYBOARD DATA

One Keyboard: Stylish Keyboard, on Google Play, by One Keyboard · Updated 6 November 2026

One Keyboard has no accounts. What you type, your clipboard, the words the keyboard learns and everything else you keep in the app stay on your phone, and we never receive them. The only information we hold is usage statistics and crash reports, linked to random IDs of the app on your phone, not to your name.

ASK US TO DELETE YOUR DATA

  1. Email hellopratiksagar@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my One Keyboard data".
  2. Tell us roughly when you installed One Keyboard and your phone's model. Because we don't know your name, this helps us find the information linked to your phone.
  3. We delete it and reply within 30 days.

WHAT WE DELETE, AND WHAT IS KEPT

  • Deleted: the usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) linked to your phone.
  • Kept: reports that don't identify anyone, such as how many people use a feature.
  • Kept under Meta's and advertising partners' own terms: setup measurement (Meta) and ad information. To stop them being linked to you, delete or reset your advertising ID: on most phones, Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Deleted anyway, without a request: usage statistics after 14 months, and crash reports after 90 days.

DELETE WHAT'S ON YOUR PHONE

You can delete these yourself at any time:

  • Personal dictionary: Settings > Personal dictionary.
  • Clipboard history and quick replies: delete clips or use "Clear recent clips" in the clipboard panel, or turn clipboard history off.
  • My details (name, phone number, emails): Settings > My details.
  • Saved places (home, work): Settings > Saved places.
  • Stickers you made: the Stickers tab. Your photo theme: Themes > Remove photo theme.
  • Everything at once: Android Settings > Apps > One Keyboard > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall One Keyboard. This also deletes the words the keyboard learned, and gives the app new random IDs, so new statistics aren't linked to the old ones.

Copies of the app's settings in your Android backup follow your Google account's backup settings.
